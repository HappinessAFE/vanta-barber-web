Our story

Built on a street that still has a forge on it

Vanta opened in 2016 in a converted metalworks in Durban, on the street where the neighbourhood's last working forge shut down the year before. We named the shop after the deepest black we could find for the sign — a nod to a trade that still gets its hands dirty. We wanted a shop that felt like that trade: deliberate, unhurried, and a little bit stubborn about doing things properly.

No chain-shop shortcuts. Every chair is run by a barber who trained for years before touching a paying client, and every cut starts with an actual conversation about what you want.
